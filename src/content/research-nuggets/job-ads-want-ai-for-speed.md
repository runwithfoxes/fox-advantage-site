If a job ad asks you to use AI, show the employer the time it saved you. Nearly half the ads we read want AI for speed.

For our report [The AI Ask](https://runwithfoxes.com/resources/the-ai-ask/2026-q3) we read 43 Irish marketing and sales job ads that ask the person to use AI tools. 20 of them say the AI is for working faster or cutting manual work. Only 7 say it is for writing or making content.

I expected writing to come first. It didn't.

Dropbox is a good example. It is hiring a sales operations manager, and the ad wants AI "to reduce manual reporting effort while maintaining strong human judgment". So it wants the speed, and it wants a person who still makes the call.

Here is what to do.

1. If you are applying, pick one task you now do faster with AI. Say how long it took before and how long it takes now.
2. Say how you checked the result.
3. If you are hiring, say in the ad what you want done faster.

One caution. 43 ads is a small number, and these are the only two reasons I counted.
