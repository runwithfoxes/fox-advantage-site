The never list

Things this site will not do. Start with what the category research found, then add your own. Examples from the course's own site: no gradients, no rounded corners on everything, no drop shadows, no stock photo of a handshake, no icon row of three boxes, no paragraph that could be on a competitor's site.

Type and colour

One typeface for headings and one for reading, or one for both. Name them. Two or three colours, named with their codes. If you have a brand guide, the answer is in it. If you do not, pick from one of the three sites you love and say so.

One thing that is only true of us

The page needs something no competitor could put on theirs: a real number, a real picture, a real promise, a real person. Write it here. If you cannot, the research is not finished.

The pictures

Where the pictures will come from. Your own photographs, a photographer, pictures made with AI from a brief. Not stock. Say which, and where they are saved.
