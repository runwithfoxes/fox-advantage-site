Making a claim with Ardline.

Nobody wants to make a claim. When you have to, we make it as straightforward as we can.

Your claim is looked after by our own claims team, from the first phone call to the final repair. We do not hand your claim to an outside company. The person you speak to works for Ardline, and their job is to get your home back to the way it was.

How it works:

  • Tell us what happened, over the phone or in your local branch
  • We appoint an assessor where one is needed, and keep you informed at every step
  • Once your claim is agreed, we arrange the repair or the payment, whichever suits

Most claims are straightforward. Where a claim is complex, we stay in touch throughout, and you will always know who is looking after it.
