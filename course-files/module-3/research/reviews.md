# reviews

> The most recent home insurance reviews for Kite's three competitors on Scorecard, a
> review platform. Saved as text on 6 Aug 2026. Scorecard is fictional, and so is every
> company, reviewer and story in this file. All of it was written for this course, in the
> register of real review platforms. "Invited: yes" marks a review the company asked for,
> usually at purchase or renewal. "Invited: no" means the reviewer came on their own.

---

## Ardline · 18 reviews · average 2.1

**1/5 · 29 Jul 2026 · Invited: no**
"Six months and counting"
Water damage claim opened in January. Still not resolved. Every phone call is a new person
and the story starts again from the beginning. The premium was never late once in eleven
years. Shameful.

**1/5 · 21 Jul 2026 · Invited: no**
"Renewal doubled for no reason"

the market and this is how they treat people.

**1/5 · 8 Jul 2026 · Invited: no**
"Storm claim refused"

than no cover because at least then you know where you stand.

Based on the other reviews here I may be the lucky one but fair is fair.
