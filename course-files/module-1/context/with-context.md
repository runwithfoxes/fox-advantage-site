## 2025 Campaign Results — Quarterly Review

**Question on the table: keep spending?**

| Channel | Spend | % of spend | New policies | % of policies | Cost per policy | Quote → policy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price comparison | €19,917 | 6% | 2,028 | 37% | **€10** | 58% |
| Brand search | €72,775 | 24% | 2,127 | 39% | **€34** | 30% |
| Podcast | €36,494 | 12% | 398 | 7% | €92 | 31% |
| Social | €96,320 | 31% | 784 | 14% | **€123** | 17% |
| Radio | €82,541 | 27% | 131 | 2% | **€630** (€316 adj.) | 33% |
| **Total** | **€308,047** | 100% | **5,468** | 100% | **€56** | 33% |

**Yes, keep spending — but not this mix.** €308k bought 5,468 policies at €56 blended. The problem is concentration: social and radio took 58% of the budget and delivered 17% of the policies, while price comparison delivered 37% of policies on 6% of spend.

**Don't assume we can just move the money to the cheap channels.** We accidentally ran that test: in Apr–May we raised price-comparison spend 60% (€351 → €559/week) and got only 3.4 extra policies a week — the incremental ones cost €62, not €10. Those channels are capped by how many people are already shopping, not by budget.

**So the cut is social, not radio.** Radio looks worst at €630, but brand search produces 43 policies in every single radio week versus 35 in every non-radio week (+7/week, zero overlap across two flights) — crediting that halo puts radio at ~€316, and it's the only lever that lifts our cheapest channel. Social has run all 52 weeks with no gap, so its €96k has never been tested at all. Recommend a 6-week dark test on social in Q1 and reallocating if policies hold.

---

Two things to discount before you compare this to plan:
- **Weeks of 10–31 Mar were free.** Brand-search and price-comparison policies roughly doubled on flat spend — about 324 policies (6% of the year) were demand, not marketing. Excluding them, true blended cost per policy is **€60**, not €56.
- **Radio's adjusted €316 is an estimate**, based on the brand-search lift above. Its directly attributed cost stands at €630.
