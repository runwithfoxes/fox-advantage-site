Kite Insurance: competitors

⚠️ Kite is a fictional brand, invented for this course, and so is every competitor named here. Ardline, Snapcover and Everly do not exist. Nothing in this document describes a real company or a real market.

Built from an interview, 26 July 2026.

Who we lose to

Ardline. The big one. Forty years in the market, sponsors things, everybody has heard of them. Wins on being the safe choice, especially with people who have had a claim before. Their line is that they have been here a long time and they pay out.

Snapcover. Price-led and comparison-site native. A quote in ninety seconds and a cheap headline number. Wins whenever the only question being asked is what it costs this year.

Everly. Sells insurance beside a current account. Nobody chooses Everly, they end up there because it was two clicks away when the reminder arrived. Wins on being already in the room.

The biggest competitor is not a company

It is the customer staying where they are. Segment 1 is thirty per cent of the market and it renews automatically every year with whoever it is already with. Nobody had to win that business and nobody is defending it.

It has no marketing budget, no brand and no weaknesses to attack, which is why it gets forgotten. A campaign that only argues against the other three is arguing with the wrong opponent.

What everyone in the category says

These are the entry fee, not a difference. Kite does not use them.

  • Trusted, or trustworthy, in any construction.
  • Here when you need us. There when it matters. Any variant.
  • We pay out. Every insurer pays out. It is the product.
  • A quote in under two minutes.
  • Save up to a percentage that nobody can verify.
  • Great service and a friendly team.

If a draft would sit unchanged on Ardline's homepage, it is a category description and it goes back.

What nobody is claiming

None of the three removes the job. They compete to win the moment the letter arrives. Kite competes to make the letter stop mattering, which is a different question and the reason the position holds.

Where they genuinely beat us

Honest, because a document without this section is a sales sheet.

  • Ardline has fame we cannot buy quickly, and branches. When something goes badly wrong, people want a building and a name they recognise.
  • Snapcover will always beat us on the headline price for a single year. Somebody comparing one number will pick them, and they should.
  • Everly has distribution we do not have. Being beside a current account beats being better.

For the writer reading this file

  1. Before using a claim, check it is not on the entry-fee list above.
  2. Staying put is the main competitor for the Payer. Write against inertia, not against a rival's price.
  3. Never name a competitor in customer-facing copy. Their claims are here for us, not for the ad.
  4. These competitors are invented for the course. Never present them as market research.
