## Rules earned today, and they cost real rounds

- ⛔ **Before rebuilding anything Paul asks for "back", grep the other
  worktrees.** "How I work" already existed, built and art-directed, in
  `.rwf-wt-prospect-pages/src/app/proposals/fidelity/FidelityDoc.tsx:286`.
- ⛔ **The rail is the four things. Three cuts in two days, always for
  busyness.** Do not add to it without Paul asking.
- ⛔ **Two rejections on one element means the FORM is wrong, not the sizes.**
  The three adoption measures took three goes. Bars that shrank to mean better,
  then a line leaving a shaded baseline, then before/now bars. The first two
  both failed the same way: they asked the reader to hold a convention before
  the picture meant anything.
- ⛔ **A `tsc` pass and a correct DOM prove nothing about the picture.** One
  render had 36 cells correctly classed and the count reading 36 with every cell
  still grey, because the colour rule was gated on a state class left over from
  an earlier cut. LOOK at every render.
- ⛔ **Never put an axis over columns that are not that axis.** A quarter
  timeline above ten columns taught the eye that across meant time, so scattered
  blue read as going back to the old way.
- ⛔ **The unverified fear statistics stay off the page.** Four survey figures in
  the Sabre research have no resolvable sources (24 Jul session record).
