Brand Guardian

Generic tools check spelling and a house style. This checks your brand.

Point it at anything you are about to publish, a deck, an ad, a page, a line of copy, and it runs the full audit against your real standards: your guidelines, your distinctive assets, your tone, your never-list. Where there is a rule it measures, to the pixel and the hex, contrast, headline width, clear-space. Where there is judgement it judges, against your brand profile, not a generic one. You get one verdict, every flag located, and the fix beside it. The bar it holds is the one a brand guardian who knew your brand would hold, built in, so nothing goes out off brand.

It runs in your own Claude and sits in front of a publish or a deploy. We build it around your brand and hand it over.
