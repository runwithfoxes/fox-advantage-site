Blueprint ● a tool Jo uses
Thin marketing to book outreach
Find Irish CEOs whose marketing team is thin, and put Paul's book in front of them on LinkedIn. Screened, written, and loaded ready to send.
Start
1
Take a slice of the pool
Net-new companies from the Irish CEO list.
2
Screen each company with Apify
How big is it, and how many of its people are in marketing.
Thin
Keep. 10 to 1,000 staff, has a marketer, but three or fewer (or under 5% of the team).
Not thin
Drop. Too big, marketing-led, or no marketer at all. Wrong buyer.
3
Write the message onto each lead
The locked book line, first name only.
Check the names first. The parser trips on titles and accents (Dr, Tomás). Sweep every first name before loading, or it sends "Hi Dr".
4
Build and load the HeyReach campaign
List, sequence, leads. All in draft.
5 Your call
Paul clicks Start
Then it sends about 25 a day on the one account.
Nothing sends until you click. Jo builds the whole thing, then stops at this line.
