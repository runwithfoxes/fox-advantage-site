What to look for

  • Scan left to right: which column makes the fox photo feel most at home?
  • Look at the accent word "thrive" in each heading. Which reads as energy, not decoration?
  • Check the dark sections: which feels like a natural evening-sky extension, not a separate component?
  • The chapter list hover state (blue title + arrow) is where you'll see the blue most often in daily use.
  • All three are far from teal (#2C7A7B). None will read as "the old palette with a tweak".
  • All three are far from Claude Design's terracotta. The original differentiation concern is resolved.
  • Orange (#F47521) stays in the logo "Run" only. Buttons and CTAs are blue.
