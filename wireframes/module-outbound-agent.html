Every cold-outreach tool does the same thing. One template, a merge field for the first name, the same email to a thousand people. The person reading it can always tell.

The Outbound Agent writes each person a real message instead. The opening is a true reaction to something they actually said, welded to a feeling, the kind of line you could not send to anyone else. If a line could appear in someone else's email unchanged, it is not done. And it runs the whole job, not just the send. It finds the right people, gets their email, reads their world, writes each one, builds the campaign across email and LinkedIn, follows up, scans the replies, and tells you who is worth a call. A marketer who did this for a living, made to run.
