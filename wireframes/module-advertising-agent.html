Advertising Agent

Making an ad is the easy part now. The hard part is the same as it always was: knowing what the ad should say, what to leave out, and what to change once it is live in front of people.

The Advertising Agent starts from your brief and your brand strategy, with twenty years of making ads that work built in. It makes the whole set a campaign needs, static, animated, brand films and video, on brand every time. Then it gets them live, watches how each one does, and decides what to keep running and what to fix. The same agent that makes the work also runs it and reads the numbers. A design tool stops at the picture. This does the job around it.

We build it around your brand and hand it over. It runs in your own Claude, and nothing goes live or spends a penny without your say so.

One brief in, a finished campaign out. These are real ads, made on brand across every format the campaign needed.
