Webinar engine
Turn one webinar into a month of on-brand content. The recording is raw material, an expert brain dump, and the engine pipes it into clips, articles, emails and more. Fast, on brand, and you stay on the final call.
Start
1
Set the theme, record in a sprint
Lock the campaign position first. Batch two or three webinars in a window, then leave the experts alone for three months.
2
Bring in the recording
Download the recording from Contrast and pull the transcript.
3 The engine reads it
It finds every usable moment
Claude reads the whole transcript against your theme and surfaces the clips, quotes, stats and angles worth using.
You pick what lands. Nothing moves on until you do, and it never invents a quote that was not said.
4
The raw material splits two ways
The same hour of talk feeds both the video clips and the written assets.
Video
Clean and brand each clip Strip the ums and the dead air, tighten, burn captions, drop in the branded frame and the hook above the speaker.
whisper transcribes word by word, ffmpeg finds and cuts the filler and pauses, then the brand machine adds the frame, captions and hook.
Written
Spin up the written assets An article in the speaker's own voice, teaser and thank-you emails, a checklist or lead magnet, sales snippets and social posts.
All drawn from what the experts actually said, in their words.
The bit that used to take days. The clip clean-up was a back and forth with an editor. It is automatic now. Contrast clips a whole segment, the engine does the surgery inside it.
5 Your review
You see everything before it goes out
Clips and written pieces come back to you in one place, at the size people will actually see them.
Nothing publishes until you say yes.
6
Schedule and publish
Out to organic, and over to sales for their own outreach.
