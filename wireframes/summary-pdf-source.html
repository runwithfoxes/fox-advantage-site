the philosophy: quality and speed

AI has made marketing production faster. That does not make the work better by default. So the order matters: we get the quality right first, then automate it. The thinking is evidence-based, not gut feel dressed up as strategy, and we build the systems rather than just advise on them.

Marketing effectiveness 4 examples
