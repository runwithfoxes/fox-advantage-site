/approach

Speed and quality, not one or the other

Most automation makes the work faster and flatter. The trick is to automate the parts that don't need judgement, so the human time goes where quality is actually made.

The usual trade is speed for quality. Faster work, worse work.

I build the engine that takes the grind out: the research, the first drafts, the measurement, the repetitive production. That frees the team to spend their time on the thinking, the taste and the judgement, the parts a machine can't do.

The result is work that ships faster and holds up better, because the people are now pointed at the part that matters.

Three ways to work together

Depending on the team and the goal, I work in one of three ways.

Ongoing

\work alongside you

For bigger teams with multiple projects. I work alongside you over time, building fluency and capability across the team rather than delivering a single thing.
