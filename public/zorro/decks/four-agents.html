The four agents, and the job each one does

Carlos
Counts the visits
1st, every Monday
What it does

Counts how many times each member came to the gym, week by week, for the last twelve weeks. He does not write any emails.

What it reads
  • Every swipe at the doordata/checkins.csv
  • The 900 members, goals and notesdata/members.csv
  • Emails sent before, and repliesdata/email_log.csv
  • Who got an email beforeagent/state/memory.json
Where it puts its work
  • A file with one line per member: visits each week, usual visits, goal and notes. Enrique reads it next.agent/out/candidates-(date).json
  • One line to say he finishedagent/out/carlos-run.log
Read Carlos’s instructions
Enrique
Writes the emails
2nd, every Monday
What it does

Looks at each member and chooses: send an email, leave them alone, or ask Marta. Then he writes the email in Marta’s voice and sends it.

What it reads
  • Carlos’s fileagent/out/candidates-(date).json
  • His own job pagestudents/enrique-role.md
  • How Marta writesmessaging/email-voice-and-rules.md
  • What to say for each goalmessaging/messages-by-goal.md
Where it puts its work
  • A file with one line per member: the choice, the reason and the email. Cato reads it next.agent/out/decisions-(date).json
  • The member’s card in Attio: emailed or ask marta, the reason, the emailAttio, People
  • Who he wrote to and when, so nobody gets two emails in four weeksagent/state/memory.json
Read Enrique’s instructions
Cato
Checks the other two
3rd, every Monday
What it does

Counts the visits again himself and makes his own choice for each member. Then he compares with Enrique and says agree or disagree, and why.

What it reads
  • Every swipe at the doordata/checkins.csv
  • The membersdata/members.csv
  • Carlos’s file and Enrique’s fileagent/out/candidates-(date).json, decisions-(date).json
  • His own job pagestudents/cato-brief.md
Where it puts its work
  • A file with one line per member: agree or disagree, and whyagent/out/cato-(date).json
  • The member’s card in Attio: his reason, and the email held for Marta when he disagreesAttio, People
Read Cato’s instructions
Rosa
Counts who came back
4th, two weeks later
What it does

Two weeks after the emails, counts how many of those members have come back to the gym. That number is what Marta wants to know.

What it reads
  • Who got an email, and whenagent/state/memory.json
  • Every swipe at the doordata/checkins.csv
  • Who opened and who replieddata/email_log.csv
Where it puts its work
  • A file: who came back, and one sentence for Marta at the topagent/out/returns-(date).json
  • The member’s card in Attio: came back or didn’t come backAttio, People
  • Who was counted, so nobody is counted twiceagent/state/memory.json
Read Rosa’s instructions
These are the four agents, like four people on staff. Each one has one job. Carlos counts the visits and writes no words. Enrique reads what Carlos made, chooses who gets an email, and sends it. Cato does the work of the other two again, on his own, and says where he disagrees. Rosa comes back two weeks later and counts who came back. Each one reads a few files and leaves its work in a place the next one can find it.
