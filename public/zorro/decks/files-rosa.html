agent/check_returns.pyThe program that runs
1"""Rosa, the fourth agent: the checking half of the Zorro agent. Two weeks after a run, did the people we wrote to come back?
2
3Reads agent/state/memory.json (who was flagged, when) and the door log, and for every member written to
4on the given Monday counts their visits in the two weeks after. Writes agent/out/returns-<date>.json and
5prints the line the owner gets.
6
7 python3 agent/check_returns.py 2026-08-31
8"""
9import csv, json, os, sys
10from collections import defaultdict
11from datetime import datetime, date, timedelta
12
13HERE = os.path.dirname(os.path.abspath(__file__)); ROOT = os.path.dirname(HERE)
14sent = date.fromisoformat(sys.argv[1]) if len(sys.argv) > 1 else date(2026, 8, 31)
15memory = json.load(open(os.path.join(HERE, 'state', 'memory.json')))
16written = {m: v for m, v in memory.items() if v.get('flagged_on') == sent.isoformat() and v.get('action') == 'draft'}
17LOG = os.path.join(HERE, 'out', 'rosa-run.log')
18def log_line(text):
19 lines = [l for l in open(LOG).read().split('\n') if l and not l.startswith(sent.isoformat())] if os.path.exists(LOG) else []
20 open(LOG, 'w').write('\n'.join(lines + [text]) + '\n')
21if not written:
22 log_line(f'{sent} NO FILE WRITTEN: nobody in memory was written to on {sent}')
23 raise SystemExit(f'nothing in memory was written to on {sent}; run the agent for that Monday first')
24visits = defaultdict(int)
25for r in csv.DictReader(open(os.path.join(ROOT, 'data', 'checkins.csv'))):
26 d = datetime.strptime(r['checkin_at'], '%Y-%m-%d %H:%M').date()
27 if sent <= d < sent + timedelta(days=14): visits[r['member_id']] += 1
28replies = {r['member_id']: r for r in csv.DictReader(open(os.path.join(ROOT, 'data', 'email_log.csv')))}
29rows = []
30for m, v in written.items():
31 rep = replies.get(m, {})
32 rows.append(dict(member_id=m, name=v.get('name'), visits_after=visits[m], returned=visits[m] > 0,
33 opened=rep.get('opened'), replied=rep.get('replied'), reply_text=rep.get('reply_text', '')))
34 memory[m]['outcome_checked_on'] = (sent + timedelta(days=14)).isoformat()
35 memory[m]['returned_within_14_days'] = visits[m] > 0
36back = sum(1 for x in rows if x['returned'])
37log_line(f'{sent} written_to={len(rows)} came_back={back} file=agent/out/returns-{sent}.json')
38json.dump(dict(sent_on=sent.isoformat(), checked_on=(sent + timedelta(days=14)).isoformat(), written_to=len(rows), came_back=back, members=rows),
39 open(os.path.join(HERE, 'out', f'returns-{sent}.json'), 'w'), indent=1, ensure_ascii=False)
40json.dump(memory, open(os.path.join(HERE, 'state', 'memory.json'), 'w'), indent=1, ensure_ascii=False)
41need_owner = [x for x in rows if x['replied'] == 'yes' and not x['returned']]
42print(f"Marta, on {sent.strftime('%d %B')} we wrote to {len(rows)} members. {back} of them have been back in the two weeks since, {round(100*back/len(rows))}%.")
43print(f"{len(need_owner)} replied and have not been in. Their replies need you, not another email:")
44for x in need_owner: print(f" {x['name']}: \"{x['reply_text']}\"")

students/rosa-role.mdThe page the program was written from
1# Rosa. The one who counts who came back
2
3The fourth of the four. Four questions, on one page. She runs two weeks after Enrique's emails went
4out and tells Marta one number: how many of the people we wrote to have been back through the door.
5Marta reads Rosa's line every time, because that number is what she wanted the whole thing for. A
6gym that sends the emails and never counts who came back does not know if any of it worked.
7
8## What Rosa produces
9
10One file, agent/out/returns-<YYYY-MM-DD>.json, where the date is the Monday the emails went out,
11not the Monday she runs. One row per member Enrique wrote to on that Monday, fields fixed:
12member_id, name, visits_after (their swipes in the fourteen days from that Monday), returned (true
13if visits_after is more than 0), opened, replied, reply_text (from the email log, or empty).
14Above the rows: sent_on, checked_on (fourteen days later), written_to (the count of rows), came_back
15(the count with returned true).
16
17And one line for Marta, printed and put at the top of the file: "On 31 August we wrote to 65
18members. 28 of them have been back in the two weeks since, 43%." Then, for every member who replied
19and has not been back, their name and their reply, because those are hers to answer, not the
20agent's.
21
22## Where it lands
23
24That file, one line in agent/out/rosa-run.log (the Monday checked, the count written to, the count
25back), and on each member's card in Attio the status moves from emailed to came back or didn't come
26back. In agent/state/memory.json each of those members gets outcome_checked_on and
27returned_within_14_days, so nobody is ever counted twice.
28
29## How she knows she is done
30
31Every member in memory flagged as a draft on that Monday has a row. came_back plus the count of
32returned false equals written_to. A second run for the same Monday gives the same file byte for
33byte and adds nothing to memory. If memory has nobody written to on that Monday, she writes no file
34and one log line saying so.
35
36## What she does not do
37
38She does not write to any member. She does not decide who to write to next; that is Carlos and
39Enrique's, next Monday, and they read her outcome from memory. She does not judge a reply; a reply
40from someone who has not been back goes to Marta, word for word. She does not read Enrique's
41reasons or emails, only who was written to and when. She runs on a Monday two weeks after the
42emails, and the runner decides when that is, not her.
43
agent/monday.pyThe runner the clock starts. It starts all four agents in order.
1"""The runner. This is what the clock starts. Nobody types this on a Monday.
2
3Runs the four agents in order and stops the moment one of them has not left its file:
4 1. Carlos prepare_week.py writes out/candidates-<monday>.json
5 2. Enrique judge.py reads that, writes out/decisions-<monday>.json
6 3. Cato cato.py reads both, writes out/cato-<monday>.json
7 4. Rosa check_returns.py two weeks after a Monday with emails, writes out/returns-<that monday>.json
8Then, if there is an Attio token, the decisions go onto the cards.
9
10Every step writes one line to state/heartbeat.log, and the last line for a Monday says done or
11where it stopped. That file is the proof it ran. A scheduler that says "loaded" proves nothing.
12
13The clock ticks every half hour (see schedule.sh). Most ticks do nothing: not a Monday, or this
14Monday is already done. That is on purpose. A laptop with the lid closed at 6am misses a 6am alarm,
15so the agent asks "has this week's Monday been done?" every time it wakes, and does it if not.
16
17 python3 agent/monday.py the most recent Monday, once
18 python3 agent/monday.py --monday 2026-09-07 a named Monday
19 python3 agent/monday.py --force run it again even if the heartbeat says done
20 python3 agent/monday.py --no-attio leave the cards alone (tests)
21"""
22import os, subprocess, sys
23from datetime import date, datetime, timedelta
24
25HERE = os.path.dirname(os.path.abspath(__file__)); ROOT = os.path.dirname(HERE)
26PY = sys.executable
27HEART = os.path.join(HERE, 'state', 'heartbeat.log')
28args = sys.argv[1:]
29force = '--force' in args
30no_attio = '--no-attio' in args or os.environ.get('ZORRO_NO_ATTIO') == '1'
31if '--monday' in args:
32 monday = date.fromisoformat(args[args.index('--monday') + 1])
33else:
34 today = date.today(); monday = today - timedelta(days=today.weekday())
35assert monday.weekday() == 0, 'the agent runs as of a Monday'
36M = monday.isoformat()
37
38def beat(text):
39 os.makedirs(os.path.dirname(HEART), exist_ok=True)
40 line = f'{datetime.now().strftime("%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S")} monday={M} {text}'
41 open(HEART, 'a').write(line + '\n'); print(line)
42
43def already_done():
44 """Done means the heartbeat says so, or the three Monday files are already there (a run from before the heartbeat existed)."""
45 if os.path.exists(HEART) and any(f'monday={M} done' in l for l in open(HEART).read().split('\n')): return True
46 return all(os.path.exists(os.path.join(HERE, 'out', f'{n}-{M}.json')) for n in ('candidates', 'decisions', 'cato'))
47
48def run(name, script, expect, extra=()):
49 """Run one agent. It is done only when the file it promised exists. Otherwise stop everything."""
50 r = subprocess.run([PY, os.path.join(HERE, script), *extra], cwd=ROOT, stdin=subprocess.DEVNULL,
51 capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=1800)
52 ok = r.returncode == 0 and os.path.exists(os.path.join(ROOT, expect))
53 tail = (r.stdout.strip().split('\n') or [''])[-1][:160] if r.stdout.strip() else (r.stderr.strip().split('\n') or [''])[-1][:160]
54 beat(f'{name} {"ok" if ok else "FAILED"} {tail}')
55 if not ok:
56 beat(f'STOPPED at {name}: {expect} was not written. Nothing after it ran.')
57 sys.exit(1)
58
59if already_done() and not force:
60 print(f'{M} is already done in {os.path.relpath(HEART, ROOT)}; nothing to do'); sys.exit(0)
61
62beat('start')
63run('carlos', 'prepare_week.py', f'agent/out/candidates-{M}.json', (M,))
64run('enrique', 'judge.py', f'agent/out/decisions-{M}.json', (M,))
65run('cato', 'cato.py', f'agent/out/cato-{M}.json', (M,))
66
67# Rosa looks back two weeks. She runs only when there was a Monday with emails then and she has not checked it yet.
68import json
69back = (monday - timedelta(days=14)).isoformat()
70memory = json.load(open(os.path.join(HERE, 'state', 'memory.json'))) if os.path.exists(os.path.join(HERE, 'state', 'memory.json')) else {}
71wrote_then = any(v.get('flagged_on') == back and v.get('action') == 'draft' for v in memory.values())
72if wrote_then and not os.path.exists(os.path.join(HERE, 'out', f'returns-{back}.json')):
73 run('rosa', 'check_returns.py', f'agent/out/returns-{back}.json', (back,))
74elif wrote_then:
75 beat(f'rosa skipped: returns-{back}.json already exists')
76else:
77 beat(f'rosa skipped: nothing was emailed on {back}')
78
79def attio(mode, when):
80 """attio.py decisions <monday> puts Enrique's reason and email on the cards; attio.py returns <monday> moves Rosa's."""
81 r = subprocess.run([PY, os.path.join(HERE, 'attio.py'), mode, when], cwd=ROOT, stdin=subprocess.DEVNULL, capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=1800)
82 tail = (r.stdout.strip() or r.stderr.strip()).split('\n')[-1][:160]
83 beat(f'attio {mode} {"ok" if r.returncode == 0 else "FAILED"} {tail}')
84 if r.returncode != 0:
85 beat(f'STOPPED at attio {mode}: the cards were not written.'); sys.exit(1)
86
87if no_attio:
88 beat('attio skipped: --no-attio')
89else:
90 attio('decisions', M)
91 attio('cato', M) # Cato's line on every card where he disagrees (17 Sep: the code existed, the runner never called it)
92 if wrote_then: attio('returns', back)
93
94beat('done')
