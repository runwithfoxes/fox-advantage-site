students/cato-brief.mdHis job page, sent to Claude
1# Cato. The red team, one page
2
3The third of the four. His job is to assume Carlos and Enrique are wrong and prove it. He is named
4after the valet Inspector Clouseau paid to attack him without warning, so he could never go soft.
5Build him on Wednesday from this page, and on Thursday afternoon we score him beside the other two.
6
7## What Cato produces
8
9One file, agent/out/cato-<YYYY-MM-DD>.json, from python3 agent/cato.py <monday>, one row per member
10id in the member file, fields fixed: member_id, cato_decision (draft, skip, ask), verdict (agree,
11disagree), why in one line quoting the data. Plus the attack list: these named checks in this order
12and no others, each with rows checked and rows failed, so a check that never ran shows 0 checked and
13cannot hide behind 0 failed: carlos_numbers, drifting_rule, joined_rule, eight_week_rule,
14written_to_rule, ask_words, skip_words, note_first_name, note_goal, note_one_ask, note_no_money.
15
16His recomputation follows Carlos's page word for word. His own decision uses Enrique's page word for
17word, taken from the page and never from Enrique's file. Any weekly count, usual rate, last-three
18count or weeks-since-last-visit that differs from his own recomputation is a disagree on that row.
19A note that fails a note check is a disagree even when the decision is right, with the failing words
20quoted. note_goal means built on the messages-by-goal line for the goal on the member's row, so a
21lose-weight note about the member's routine passes. A quote is the weekly counts, the date of the
22last swipe, or the words from the notes or reply, never more than a line. Counts, dates and the
23first-name and money checks are code; the goal and one-ask checks and the words in why are the model.
24
25## Where it lands
26
27That file, and the field cato_check on the member's record in Attio: the Monday and his line where he
28disagrees, empty where he agrees. Marta writes right or wrong into cato_verdict on the same record.
29Cato never writes the note.
30
31## How he knows he is done
32
33One row for every member id, the attack list present with all eleven checks, and two runs on the same
34Monday give the same verdict on every row. If memory.json, Carlos's file or Enrique's file is missing,
35no file and one log line naming the path.
36
37## What he does not do
38
39He never repairs a decision or rewrites a note. He does not read Enrique's file before he has his own
40view from Carlos's file and the data. A goal is not a note: "rehab after injury" on the goal line is
41why the member joined, and only the notes or the last reply can say injured today. An empty cell in
42Carlos's file is not a difference; only a number that differs from his own is.
43
44## How to attack
45
46The door log is data/checkins.csv, the member file data/members.csv, the email log
47data/email_log.csv, the write-to history agent/state/memory.json. Written to means what Enrique's
48page says: a draft or ask in memory with flagged_on before the Monday and within 28 days. If he
49drafted, what would make it a skip or an ask. If he skipped, what would make it a drift. If he asked,
50what in the notes or the reply put it in Marta's hands, quoted; if nothing there does, disagree.
51
52## Why he exists
53
54One separate check by an agent with a fresh start is the biggest measured gain in the research on
55agent teams. And a checker that logs only what broke cannot be trusted when it is quiet, because a
56row nobody opened looks the same as a row that held. That is why the attack list is half of what he
57produces.

1# The emails. Marta's voice and the rules the agent follows
2
3The students get this file. They do not have to work out the positioning or the messaging; that
4would eat the week. Paul talks about why the quality of these emails matters. The agent has to

6
7## Who is writing
8
9Marta Ferrer, the owner. She is writing to one person she knows, not to a list. She has noticed
10they have not been in. She is not selling anything and she is not guilt-tripping anyone.
11
12## What every email does
13
14It says who it is from, in the first line, and that Marta noticed. It names the thing the member
15said they wanted, in their own words off the record. It makes one ask, small and specific: a class
16this week, a morning, a reply. It ends. Four or five sentences. Under eighty words.
17
18## What an email never does
19
20It never says "we miss you". It never mentions the membership fee, the contract, or renewal. It
21never asks why they stopped. It never offers a discount. It never lists three things they could do.
22It never uses the word journey. It never guesses at a reason the record does not give.
23
24It never names the member's private life. No wedding, no holiday, no family member, no health
25matter, no date. A gym writing about someone's sister's wedding is intrusive, however well meant.
26The email refers to the goal the member gave when they joined in general words only: "what you
27were working towards", "the goal you set when you joined". Warm, but professional. Paul, 11 Sep 2026.
28
29## When the agent does not write
30
31The record says away, on holiday, travelling. The record says injured, physio, surgery, doctor,
32pregnant. The member joined less than four weeks ago. The member has been gone eight weeks or
33more. The member has always come once a fortnight. A reply has come in that the agent cannot judge.
34In every one of those cases the agent writes nothing and tells Marta why, in one line.

1"""The runner. This is what the clock starts. Nobody types this on a Monday.
2
3Runs the four agents in order and stops the moment one of them has not left its file:
4 1. Carlos prepare_week.py writes out/candidates-<monday>.json
5 2. Enrique judge.py reads that, writes out/decisions-<monday>.json
6 3. Cato cato.py reads both, writes out/cato-<monday>.json
7 4. Rosa check_returns.py two weeks after a Monday with emails, writes out/returns-<that monday>.json
8Then, if there is an Attio token, the decisions go onto the cards.
9
10Every step writes one line to state/heartbeat.log, and the last line for a Monday says done or
11where it stopped. That file is the proof it ran. A scheduler that says "loaded" proves nothing.
12
13The clock ticks every half hour (see schedule.sh). Most ticks do nothing: not a Monday, or this
14Monday is already done. That is on purpose. A laptop with the lid closed at 6am misses a 6am alarm,
15so the agent asks "has this week's Monday been done?" every time it wakes, and does it if not.
16
17 python3 agent/monday.py the most recent Monday, once
18 python3 agent/monday.py --monday 2026-09-07 a named Monday
19 python3 agent/monday.py --force run it again even if the heartbeat says done
20 python3 agent/monday.py --no-attio leave the cards alone (tests)
21"""

23from datetime import date, datetime, timedelta

30no_attio = '--no-attio' in args or os.environ.get('ZORRO_NO_ATTIO') == '1'

46 return all(os.path.exists(os.path.join(HERE, 'out', f'{n}-{M}.json')) for n in ('candidates', 'decisions', 'cato'))

48def run(name, script, expect, extra=()):
49 """Run one agent. It is done only when the file it promised exists. Otherwise stop everything."""
50 r = subprocess.run([PY, os.path.join(HERE, script), *extra], cwd=ROOT, stdin=subprocess.DEVNULL,
51 capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=1800)
52 ok = r.returncode == 0 and os.path.exists(os.path.join(ROOT, expect))
53 tail = (r.stdout.strip().split('\n') or [''])[-1][:160] if r.stdout.strip() else (r.stderr.strip().split('\n') or [''])[-1][:160]
54 beat(f'{name} {"ok" if ok else "FAILED"} {tail}')
55 if not ok:
56 beat(f'STOPPED at {name}: {expect} was not written. Nothing after it ran.')
