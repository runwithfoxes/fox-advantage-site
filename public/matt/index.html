Case study / FMCG

New packaging
size increases
market share

We partnered with Mexican home-cleaning brand PINOL to test how larger pack sizes could strengthen their market position. The answer was clear.

Client PINOL
Industry FMCG
Solution Product range optimisation
/01 the client

A brand with
over 1,000 uses.

Since 1953, PINOL has established itself as a leading, reliable, and innovative brand capable of tackling any cleaning challenge thanks to its powerful pine oil formula. It is an indispensable ally in home cleaning, and the only cleaner with over 1,000 proven uses.

Concerned about the health and well-being of their customers, PINOL has been able to establish an emotional connection with them by providing quality, efficiency and cleanliness with products that are safe to use.

/02 the challenge

Everybody else
had a bigger pack.

On the Mexican market, most house cleaning brands offered both a smaller (~1L) and a larger (~1.5L) product version. PINOL did not. We needed to find out how introducing a bigger packaging option would influence their sales performance and market share.

01

The gap

We wanted to find out the impact of introducing a larger packaging option for PINOL.

02

The method

A standard choice-based conjoint featuring PINOL and competing products was used.

03

The result

Introducing the larger pack would lead to category growth and a bigger market share for PINOL.

/03 the solution

Choice-based
conjoint.

A standard choice card conjoint survey was used to quickly identify the product variation loyalty in the instance of replacement, and packaging size loyalty in the case of incremental listing.

The survey compared PINOL products with their key competitors in terms of different size options (~1L and ~1.5L) and several price variations, to determine customer preference and how they could use this information to optimise their product range.

The results were collected and analysed within 3 business days.
