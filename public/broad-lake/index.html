// who they are

A growth partner, not a passive investor

Broadlake is a 100% independent Irish firm that takes a stake in businesses with room to grow, then works alongside their leaders to get them there. The model pairs capital with a platform of tools and processes built up over more than a decade.

It is led by Pete Smyth, who built and ran the facilities group Noonan Services before founding the firm in 2008. Decisions are made in-house, without outside pressure on the timeline.

"We are not just building successful businesses, we are building a movement."

// the approach
