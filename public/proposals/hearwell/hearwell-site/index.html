hEARsphere™

Not a hearing test. A healthy-ageing assessment.

Every other clinic in Ireland asks one question: can you hear this tone? We measure ten things across four pillars of how you are ageing, and give you a number you can improve.

Pillar 01

Social & emotional

Confidence in company, the conversations you have stopped having, and the ones you now avoid.

Pillar 02

Physical

Balance, fatigue and sleep, all of which hearing effort quietly takes from you.

Pillar 03

Auditory

Audibility tested to 12,500 Hz, against an industry standard of 8,000 Hz.

Pillar 04

Cognitive

Memory and listening effort, measured rather than guessed at.
