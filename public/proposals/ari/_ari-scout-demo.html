Scout

Scout is a trigger-based outbound engine. It watches a curated list of the colleges ARI would most like as customers, and it reaches out at the moment a genuine buying-window signal appears. One college at a time, on the right channel, never as a blast. It watches for the opening, and takes it.

The thesis behind it is straightforward. In cold outreach, the lever is timing and targeting rather than clever words. A live signal tells you that a college is in the market now, and that is the window Scout is built to catch. ARI already holds the list of roughly 1,000 realistic colleges, and Ben has already named the four signals that matter: a new athletic director is appointed, a president or VP of enrolment starts asking the athletics department for recruiting funnel data it cannot produce, a recruiting or athletics administration role is advertised, or a competitor raises its price at renewal or is acquired and its product stops moving forward. ARI has already won a customer through that last signal, when a large competitor raised prices at the end of a three-year contract.

Scout decides who to contact and when. It does not write the message. Once a college is approved, Scout hands it to the Outbound Agent, which does the research and writes that college its own message. The two work as one pipeline. In most weeks, most of the list produces no signal, and nothing is sent to those colleges. Ben approves the shortlist before anything goes out.
