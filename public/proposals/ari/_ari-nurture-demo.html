The 95 Engine

Around 1,000 colleges make up ARI's realistic market, and ARI already holds the list. At any given time only a small number of them are looking to buy an athletic CRM. The other 950 will not be ready for a year or longer. Keeping those 950 informed and interested over that period, without contacting them so often that they stop paying attention, is a real job, and it normally requires a hire, an agency, or a freelancer. The 95 Engine does that job.

It monitors the list for the events that change a college's situation, writes the one message that is genuinely useful to that college given where it is, and then builds the flows and triggers inside ARI's own Klaviyo or HubSpot, on ARI's subscription and in ARI's name. The writing is where the quality sits, and it is specific to the reader. A newly appointed athletic director receives a different email to a college president who has started asking for recruiting data.

The system is designed to contact a college only when there is a good reason to. In many months it will decide that no message is warranted, and it will send nothing. This is deliberate, because contacting a college repeatedly when nothing has changed reduces the chance they will pay attention when something has. Ben reviews all of the content before any flow goes live.
