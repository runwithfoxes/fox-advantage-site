ARI Athletics
Recruiting and management software for collegiate student-athletes
Most athletic departments do not have a recruiting problem. They have a handover problem. The coach knows the prospect. The admissions office knows the applicant. For most of the cycle those are two different people in two different systems, and nobody notices until an offer is on the table and the file does not match the conversation. That is the gap we built ARI to close. One CRM for coaches and athletic administrators, with a real-time, bidirectional link into Slate, so athletics and admissions are reading the same record on the same day. The recruiting gets easier once the handover stops being a handover.

The handover is where recruiting is won or lost

Coaches and admissions officers are usually looking at the same student-athlete and seeing two different people. The forms sit in one place, the messages in another, and the metrics somewhere else again. Connecting athletics and admissions is not a systems tidy-up. It is the difference between a prospect who feels known and one who feels processed. Here is what changes when both sides work from one record, and what to look at first if yours are still apart.
