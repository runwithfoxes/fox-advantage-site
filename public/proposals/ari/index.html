What I'd recommend

What we would build for ARI

This comes from our conversation. ARI sells to a defined market: roughly a thousand US colleges are a realistic fit, out of about two thousand with athletics programmes, and you already hold that list. Every college publishes its athletics staff directory publicly, so you can also identify the right people at each one without buying data. That is a strong starting position, and it shapes what we would do. Because the list is finite, the work is not about reaching more people. It is about reaching the right people at the right moment, and being familiar to the rest so that when they do start looking, ARI is already a name they recognise. Below is what we would build, in the order we would build it. Each piece is configured for ARI, then handed to your team to run.

First · the thinking

Positioning and messaging

We would start by agreeing how ARI is described and sold, because every other piece of work depends on it. This means deciding what ARI does that the alternatives do not, which colleges it is for, and how to answer a buyer who is comparing you against a larger platform or against doing nothing. We write that down as a short set of messages, and everything we build afterwards uses them.

You already have the substance for this. ARI works only with college athletic departments, and only at Division III and NAIA level. Support is close because the company is small. The price does not jump at renewal. None of that is currently on your website, and the line you lead with, level up your recruiting, could be used by any competitor. The stronger position is the one you described on our call: athletics and admissions usually work from separate records, and ARI is the system that puts them on the same one.

What this covers
  • How ARI is describedA short, agreed statement of what the product does and who it is for, written so that a coach or an athletic director can repeat it accurately.
  • Which colleges we targetDivision III and NAIA, and the profile within that which is worth pursuing first.
  • How we answer the alternativesWhat to say when a college is comparing ARI with a larger platform, with a competitor, or with keeping things as they are.
  • The messages themselvesWritten once and then used by the outreach, the website and the content, so that everything says the same thing.

Two issues we found while building it
  • Your email domain and your website domain are differentYou send email from arirecruiting.com, and your website resolves to ARI Athletics. A college that receives a message from one and arrives at the other has to work out that they are the same company. It is a small point, but it costs you credibility on a first approach.
  • www.ariathletics.com does not loadThe security certificate does not cover the www version of the address, so anyone typing it sees a browser security warning. The address without www works normally. This is a straightforward fix and worth making regardless of whether we work together.

How to start

Two options

Option A is the smaller piece of work, and it is designed to generate enquiries from the colleges that are actively looking now. Option B includes all of Option A and adds the work that builds familiarity with the colleges that are not yet looking, so that ARI is considered when they are. We would recommend starting with Option A. It is quicker, the results are measurable, and it gives you a clear view of how we work before committing to the larger programme.
