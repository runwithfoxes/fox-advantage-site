The session

What we would work through

This comes from your emails and our conversation in June. You have three pieces of work in front of you that you think these tools should be able to help with: a plan for taking the new pet range into retail, video and social content built from footage you already own, and the year-to-date spend against annual budget. You also said the thing that matters most for this session, which is that you want to cover the basics first and then be specific about what you need. So we work on Mervue's own files rather than examples, and by the end the team can tell the difference between what these tools do well and what they do badly. Below is what we would cover.

The half day, in order
  • Where these tools are strong, and where they breakI ran generation tests on your Skin & Coat sachet last week. The tool re-lettered your wordmark and printed "Est. 1026" instead of 1986, and produced the gel as a rigid ribbed cylinder rather than the soft blob it is. We start here, because a team that knows the failure modes checks the right things and stops trusting output that looks finished.
  • The retail launchWe brief the plan for taking the pet range into retail properly, then look at what comes back that is useful and what comes back generic. This is the one you said you were struggling to outline, and you would leave the day with a real start on it.
  • Written work at volumeAmazon listing copy, briefs for distributors and retail accounts, and export-language versions for Turkish, Spanish and your other markets.
  • Social and video, from what you already ownYour five cat clips and your eight Amazon listing images. What can be produced from footage and artwork you already have, what still needs your designer, and how to tell those apart before committing to a piece of work.
  • The numbersWe build the year-to-date spend against annual budget analysis during the session, so the method is yours to repeat each month rather than a file I hand over.
  • Staying on brand when more than one person is making thingsYour logo and its clear space, your colours, your tone and your product claims, written down once in a form these tools can follow. This is the practical answer to distributors and retailers producing their own off-brand versions.

Why it is priced this way

This session's job is to let you decide what happens next, so I have priced it to be an easy decision rather than a budget exercise. It is deliberately a small commitment.

Some of what the map points at, your team will do in-house once they have seen how. Some of it you may want built for you, and the brand consistency piece in particular tends to be the one companies want help with, because it only works if it is set up properly once. That is a conversation for after the day, when you know what you are asking for.
