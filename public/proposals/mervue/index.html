The session

What we would cover

You said you wanted to cover the basics first, and then be specific about what you need. The difficulty with that is that the basics of AI in marketing are wider than they look, and it is hard to know what to ask for while you can only see part of it.

So the half day is structured on the six parts of my AI fluency course, and I take examples from each. We would not run the whole course in an afternoon, and this is not that. The structure is there because it gives you the full picture of what AI fluency in marketing covers, from the daily habits through to the things nobody could do at all until recently. By the end your team can see the whole territory, and you can see which parts of it matter to Mervue.

Where it helps I point the examples at the kind of work your team does, so the ideas land against something familiar rather than a generic example. The session itself is about the fluency, not about any one piece of Mervue work.

01

The 80/20 of AI

A small number of habits get you most of the way with AI in marketing work. Talking to it instead of typing. Keeping a campaign in one project instead of scattered chats. Showing it the content you liked rather than describing it.

02

Slow, then fast

An intentional way of setting about marketing work. Techniques for getting clear on what good looks like, for fast research, for the metrics, for building in defences against hallucination. All of it before the work starts, so what follows is quick and consistently good.

03

Create adjacent value

A deep dive into the wide range of marketing capabilities you can now build with AI, and how to get them to a genuinely competent standard. No need to stay specialised as a brand, performance, product or research marketer. You add to what you are already good at.

04

System thinking

Once you can create adjacent value, you can build the whole system. A website, a chatbot, a calendar, the email, the research that lands before a call. Wired together into one experience for the customer, end to end.

05

Building marketing agents

The processes, structures and tools I've used to build agents that do real marketing work, whether that is research, outbound, writing emails or coaching a brief. Getting the workflow right, and getting the quality right.

06

Tackle difficult problems

The other modules are about doing your work faster and better. This one is about the marketing problems and opportunities that were never possible at all, even for a big team. Now is the time to be ambitious and take them on.
