Outbound Agent

Outbound is a job a person does. Someone has to work through the register of charities, find the CEO at each one, learn enough about that organisation to have something worth saying, and then write to them properly. Yellow Harbour has done this by hand, with interns working through the register. It helped double the business, but it is slow, and doing it at volume has traditionally meant a full-time hire, an agency, or a freelancer. The Outbound Agent carries out that whole job, and it writes every message individually.

Your sweet spot is the SME charity with an income between €500,000 and €1 million, where the CEO holds the grants themselves. The agent filters the register to that profile, identifies the CEO, and researches the organisation and the person: the filed accounts, the grants and funders they already list, the campaigns they are running, and what the CEO has said publicly. Every message is written from that research, so it could not have been sent to anyone else.

It works across LinkedIn and email. Emails go one at a time from your own mailbox, never as a bulk blast, and your Mailchimp newsletter stays as it is. Louise or David reads every message in full, and nothing is sent and nothing is spent until you approve it. We build the agent around your messaging framework, hand it over, and it runs in your own Claude account.

You give the agent a single instruction, for example "write to fifty charity CEOs from the register this week". It filters the register to your profile, identifies the CEO at each charity, researches the organisation and the person, and writes a message from what it found.
