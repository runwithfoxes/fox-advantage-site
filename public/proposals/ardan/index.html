How I work

Quality first, then automate

Ireland's Marketer of the Year, 2022

Before I build anything, I ask one question: what does really good look like here? Not what AI can do, but what the best version of this marketing would be, and the level of quality and effectiveness I would want to stand over.

So I start where I always have. If there were no AI at all, what team would I hire to do this properly? An email marketer, a copywriter for each property, someone to build and run the websites, someone running the whole calendar. The capabilities, the craft and the resources it would genuinely take to be good. I map that team first, the one I would build in a world before any of this existed.

Then I build exactly that, with agents instead of hires. The quality bar is set by the team I would have wanted, not by whatever a tool happens to make easy. Twenty years in brand is what tells me where that bar sits: Head of Brand at O2 Ireland, then CMO at the National Lottery, Head of Brand at Indeed and Miro, both global roles. Positioning, messaging and tone written first, then built into everything the agents make.

What I'd recommend

Five weeks in, eighteen properties, very little structure

Everything here comes from our conversation. A newly formed group, eighteen properties from pubs with rooms to the flagship houses, a near-empty mailing list, and a team of four already stretched, one of them full-time just keeping websites and booking engines in step. You need structure across the whole group, fast, without ever losing the human welcome that makes the places what they are. Here are the products I'd recommend, each configured for Ardán and handed to your team.

Agent · configured for Ardán Collective

Website Builder

In a world before AI, a new property site meant a web designer, a front-end developer and a producer to manage it. The Website Builder is that whole role in one: it takes the brief, does the UX and the UI, builds the pages to your brand, and pushes them live to hosting. Booking stays exactly as it is, on Avvio, untouched.

Once a property's design rules are decoded up front, a new page, a seasonal offer or a fixed typo is a sentence, not a ticket. You watched a version of this on our call: you named a property, and a few minutes later a Bellinter page was live.

How to start

Build the whole system, or start with one or two

Two ways in, and both leave you owning what we build, in your own tools, in your name. My honest recommendation is to start small: a proof of concept on a single brand, the website and the lifecycle emails that only really work when they work together, then scope the full department from what you learn.
