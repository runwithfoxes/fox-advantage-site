Outbound Agent

Outbound is a job a person does. Someone has to build the list of companies, work out which of them are worth approaching, find the right person inside each one, learn enough about that company to have something worth saying, and then write to that person properly across four emails and three LinkedIn touches. Doing that properly at any volume has traditionally meant a full-time hire, an agency, or a freelancer. In practice most companies end up with a template and a few fields swapped in, because nobody has the hours. The Outbound Agent does that whole job, and it writes every message individually.

The part that takes the hours is not the part that needs the skill. Pulling the list, checking each company against the ICP, hunting for the right role, finding the LinkedIn profile and the work email, reading the funding announcement and the product page, then copying it all back into HubSpot by hand: that is the grind, and it is what currently takes two or three hours an evening. The part that decides whether the message works is the writing, and the judgement about who is worth writing to at all. The agent takes the grind off you entirely and does the research and the writing for each person, one at a time. You keep the judgement, because you approve the list and you read the messages before anything sends.

The agent runs both channels, email and LinkedIn, in the four-plus-three sequence you already use. It connects to HubSpot through its API and loads each message there, so HubSpot stays the system of record and the sequencing engine. It pauses for your approval at three points: when the companies and the roles are chosen, when the messages are written, and before anything is sent. Nothing is sent and nothing is spent until you approve it. We would build it around Santegic's positioning in regulatory compliance and cybersecurity, hand it over, and train you to run it. It would live in your own Claude account.

This agent sits alongside Scout, and the two do different jobs. Scout decides who to contact and when. The Outbound Agent researches and writes the message.

You give the agent a single instruction, for example "work through this week's health-tech and medtech companies and write to the right person at each one". It finds that person, finds their LinkedIn profile and work email, researches the company and the individual, and writes each message from what it found.
