Ghostwriter

A content writer or a ghostwriter used to mean a full-time hire, an agency, or a freelancer. The grind in the job is the volume and the consistency, which is where most companies fall down, and it is why the posts and the SEO content currently get squeezed in around everything else. The quality is in the argument and the accuracy, and in a regulated field the accuracy has to be right. Santegic knows more about this subject than the people you are writing for, and almost none of that knowledge currently reaches them.

Ghostwriter writes LinkedIn posts for Larry and Marie-Claire, in their own voices, on the subjects you already deal with every week: what a new regulation actually means for a health-tech founder in plain English, how the medical device classification process works and where companies go wrong early, where the line sits between a wellness product and a clinical claim, why the right time to sort compliance is long before you think you need to, and the cybersecurity obligations that come with holding patient data. It works from what you have already written and said, so the posts argue what Santegic argues rather than drifting into generic content.

The reason to do it is the one you already give your own clients when you tell them the best time to come to you is well before they think they need you. Most of the companies who will eventually need Santegic are not looking this quarter, which is exactly what your outbound replies were telling you. Writing that is genuinely useful to those people means that when a regulation lands or an investor asks a hard question, Santegic is a name they have already read and already trust, and in a trust-led category that is what gets you the call. Nothing publishes without Larry or Marie-Claire approving it, and the system does not invent statistics, clients or regulatory facts.
