First · the thinking

The 95 and the 5

This is the principle we talked about on the call, and it is worth setting out properly because it explains why the four products below are the ones we would build. In any category, only about five percent of your potential customers are in the market to buy in a given quarter. The rest are not. They may well be a good fit for Santegic, but right now they are busy, or they have someone else, or the deadline that will force the issue has not arrived yet. When you ran outbound and most of the replies said they were busy, that was not a failure of the message. That was the ninety-five percent answering honestly.

The part that matters commercially is what happens when one of them moves into the five percent. By the time a company decides it needs regulatory help, it usually already has a short list in its head, and that list is made up of the names it has heard of. If Santegic has done nothing to be known in the meantime, the first approach lands as a cold call from a stranger, and it is competing against a firm the buyer already recognises. You described this yourself, twice, without needing the theory. You said you want people to know about you before they need you, and you tell your own clients that the best time to come to Santegic is well before they think they need to.

So the work has two halves and we would do both. We go after the five percent who are looking now, through outreach that is properly researched and properly written. At the same time we build familiarity with the ninety-five percent who are not looking yet, so that when their deadline arrives Santegic is already a name they know. In a category as trust-led as yours, where you told me people need to half know you before they will talk, that second half is not a nice to have.

How to start

Two options

Option A is the smaller piece of work, and it goes after the companies that are looking now. It is the outreach, running properly on both LinkedIn and email, so that the evening research and the copy-paste between Prospeo, Claude and HubSpot stop being your job. Option B includes all of Option A and adds the work that builds familiarity with the companies that are not looking yet, so that Santegic is a name they already know when their deadline arrives. We would recommend starting with Option A. It is quicker, you can see whether it brings enquiries in, and it gives you a clear view of how we work before you commit to the larger programme.
