Campaign Manager

The Campaign Manager is the layer that runs the other agents and drives the tools you already pay for. Keeping outbound moving is a job in itself. Someone has to load the sequence into HubSpot, work through the LinkedIn tasks HubSpot creates, copy drafts back and forth between Claude and HubSpot, and keep track of what is actually getting replies. That work used to need a full-time campaign or marketing manager, or an agency retainer. At the moment it is Marie-Claire, in the evenings.

This does not replace HubSpot. As Paul put it on the call, it would not replace HubSpot, it would replace the person who has to look after HubSpot. You are on HubSpot Professional at about €140 a month, upgraded mainly to get email sequencing, and you are committed for the year. Our honest read is that it is over-specced for what you use it for and there are cheaper tools, but you are locked in, so we connect to it through its API and drive it rather than ask you to rip anything out.

What is left for you and Larry is the judgement: which prospects are worth a real conversation, what to run next, and what to stop. Nothing sends and nothing spends until one of you approves it. The aim we discussed on the call is that running outbound becomes about an hour of your week rather than a full-time job.

This is the board Marie-Claire and Larry would open. It is the one place where you can see what the agents are doing, what is waiting on you, and what is working. The campaigns, dates and figures below are illustrative, put there to show how the board reads. They are not Santegic results.
