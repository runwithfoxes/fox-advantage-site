Campaign Manager

The Campaign Manager is where a marketing plan stops being a document and starts running. It plans the campaigns that build Data Intelligence's name on the cost-control conversation, makes the work with the Ghostwriter, and tracks how every piece is doing, in one place your team approves from.

This is where you see the whole operation at once. What went out, what landed, what needs a decision this week, without a status meeting to find out. Today it runs the Ghostwriter. Add an Outbound Agent or an Ads Agent later and it tracks those too. It is a system composed of the other agents, and it becomes your one source of truth, with the dashboards to match.

What makes it more than a dashboard is that it refers to your positioning, messaging and tone before it does anything, so every plan is grounded in Data Intelligence's own strategy, not a generic template. Your junior marketer runs the approvals day to day, with me alongside as your fractional CMO, tuning and steering it. We build it around your brand and hand it over.
