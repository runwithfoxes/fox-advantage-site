The groundwork · before any agent

The foundation

Before a single agent gets built, we do the thinking. What does Data Intelligence stand for, who is it for, and which moments does it want to own? In a world before AI, this is the work you would hire a brand strategist and a CMO to do. It is the part that makes everything after it good.

You want to be the name that comes to mind the moment a company realises its technology spend is out of control. That does not happen by accident. We decide the position, write the messaging, and set the marketing strategy, so the agents are not guessing. They run a plan, in a voice, against a set of triggers we have chosen on purpose.
