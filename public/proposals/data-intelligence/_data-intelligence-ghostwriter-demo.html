Ghostwriter

Data Intelligence has a point of view worth hearing, and a founder who has watched the problem up close: technology spend that grows unchecked because no one truly owns it. That point of view is worth more the more people hear it.

But authority only compounds if the point of view actually gets published, and that takes Dave's time plus a ghostwriter most new companies do not have. Ghostwriter takes Dave's view, from a voice note or from what he has already written, and turns it into a steady stream of posts and a newsletter, written the way he would say it. Provocative enough to get noticed, authoritative enough to be trusted, and grounded in what he has actually seen running real technology projects.

LinkedIn is the first touch, where the name gets built. The newsletter is the channel that lasts: the people who follow the argument move from a feed onto a list Data Intelligence owns. It never invents a view or a claim. It publishes his.

Dave's view on why technology costs run out of control, shaped into his own words. The angle and the voice here are illustrative: the real ones come out of the positioning work. This shows the machine. Here is the draft, ready for his review, and the same idea as a newsletter issue.
