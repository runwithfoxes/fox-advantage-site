Outbound Agent

Outbound is a job a person does. Someone has to pick the companies worth approaching, find the HR leader in each one, research the company, and write to that person. Doing it properly at any scale has needed a full-time hire, an agency, or a freelancer. Most outbound ends up as a template with a few fields swapped in. The Outbound Agent does the whole job, and it writes each message separately.

The agent builds the target list rather than buying one: mid-market employers of five hundred to five thousand staff, in the sectors Nova already works in. It finds the HR leader at each company, and the finance and IT people too where the decision is shared. It then researches the company and the person: staff numbers, how many countries, which HR system they run, whether payroll sits inside it, recent acquisitions, how long the person has been in the job, and what they have said publicly. Each message is written from that research.

The agent runs on email and LinkedIn, and it handles Ireland and the US differently because the law is different. Irish contacts are emailed one at a time from Cian's own mailbox, which keeps that side GDPR compliant. US contacts can be emailed in volume. There are three approval points: the companies and roles, the messages, and the send. Nothing goes out and nothing is spent until you approve it. The agent is built around Nova's positioning and messaging, handed over, and runs in your own Claude account.

You give the agent one instruction, for example "find mid-market employers who have outgrown their HR system and write to the HR leader at each one". It finds the person, researches the company, and writes the message. Below is the same business put to three different people.
