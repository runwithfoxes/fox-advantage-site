Ghostwriter

For an HR leader to know Nova before they start looking for a system, someone has to publish regularly and publish something worth reading. That has meant hiring a content person, an agency or a freelancer. None of those work well here. You know the market and have no time to write. Writers with the time do not know the market. You said on the call that you are not short of things to say, you are just not consistent.

You already produce the material. You speak at UNLEASH and the HR technology shows, you ran a two hour workshop with fifteen people last week, you have the same conversation at every conference, and you run diagnostic calls most weeks. None of it gets written down. Ghostwriter takes what you already say, from a voice note or a recording of a talk, and turns it into LinkedIn posts, articles for the website and short video clips, in your voice rather than a generic company tone. You do not have to write anything.

You know how to use these tools yourself. This adds two things. It runs your positioning and messaging every time, so everything is consistent. And it runs on a cadence without you having to find the time. Only a small share of your market is looking for a system in any quarter. The rest come later, when a contract is up for renewal, a new HR leader inherits a bad setup, or the board asks for something the system cannot produce. Publishing consistently means Nova is a name they know by then. Nothing is invented, no client is named without their written agreement, and you approve every piece before it goes out.

Rather than describe this, we wrote a sample. The draft below comes from something you said on the call, about the gap between what the vendors sell and what your clients are dealing with. It would go to you for review before it went anywhere, and the same subject is shown developed further. It is here to show how the system works. The subjects we would publish would come out of the brand strategy work.
