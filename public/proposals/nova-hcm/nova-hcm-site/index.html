We are growing fast
Growth is where an HR stack stops coping quietly. Systems that were fine at one size start needing people to hold them together, and the cost shows up as admin rather than as a line in the budget. The useful first move is a diagnostic that says what you run today, what it costs and what it is actually used for. That is a conversation with one of our HR technology advisors.

Our systems do not talk
Systems that do not talk are rarely a technology fault. They are the result of sensible decisions taken one at a time, each one fine on its own. The fix starts with mapping the joins and the manual work sitting in them, then deciding what stays. The fastest way in is a short conversation with one of our HR technology advisors.

Choosing a new HRIS is the easy half. Adoption, stakeholder alignment and change management decide whether it lands, and that is where most timelines go.
