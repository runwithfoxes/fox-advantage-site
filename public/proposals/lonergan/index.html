How I work

Quality first, then automate

Ireland's Marketer of the Year, 2022

Before I build anything, I ask one question: what does really good look like here? Not what AI can do, but what the best version of this marketing would be, and the level of quality and effectiveness I would want to stand over.

So I start where I always have. If there were no AI at all, what would it take to do this properly? For the thing you need most right now, which is new business, it would take someone whose whole job is finding the right companies, working out who to talk to inside each one, and writing to each of those people well enough that it does not read like a mailshot. Done properly that is a full-time role, and a skilled one. I map that role first, the person I would want doing this if none of these tools existed.

Then I build exactly that, with an agent instead of a hire. The quality bar is set by the person I would have wanted, not by whatever a tool happens to make easy. Twenty years in brand is what tells me where that bar sits: Head of Brand at O2 Ireland, then CMO at the National Lottery, Head of Brand at Indeed and Miro, both global roles. Positioning, messaging and tone written first, then built into everything the agent makes. It is the same care you put into your own work, the Pantone match and the note from the chief executive in the box, applied to the outreach.

When you are ready

The same setup builds the rest of the team

We start with the LinkedIn agent because it is the quickest way to bring cash in, and because once you have it running you and your team will understand how all of this works. When Christmas is behind you and there is room to build, the same tools and the same approach add the rest, one piece at a time. Each of these is a separate step, taken only when it earns its place. None of it is part of the training session, and none of it is needed to start.

Close to the outreach
  • Email alongside LinkedInThe same agent working your HubSpot list and cold email as well as LinkedIn, so the same buyer is reached on more than one channel. Cold email to Irish prospects needs care under data protection rules, so we would set how that runs together.
  • A copywriter for your quotes and proposalsThe quotes and follow ups Ava puts together by hand, written to your standard in a fraction of the time, so the sales admin stops eating the day.
  • A search agentThe job your old SEO did, running every day rather than falling off a cliff, and covering how Lonergan shows up in ChatGPT and other AI answers, which is where a lot of search is going.
A bigger step
  • A website you controlRebuilt so a new page, or a landing page for a client who has just replied, takes minutes instead of a development cycle, and so you are never again clicking between separate tools just to change something.
  • Looking after the clients you already haveThe client and staff journeys, from the first order to the reorder, handled so that your existing customers, who already stay with you, are looked after without it becoming another job.

How this works

What I would do, and what it costs you

You said the budget is tight and what you need is cash in before Christmas, so this is built to get you moving without a bill from me. I would set the agent up and train your team to run it, at no charge. The only thing you pay for is the software, which is yours and stays in your name. Here is exactly how it would go.

Before we meet
I set it up

I spend around 90 minutes with you capturing how Lonergan is described, sold and positioned, and turn it into a short messaging framework, the standard the agent writes from. Then I build the agent and connect it to your tools, so it is ready to run. A few hours of my time, before the session.

The session
Two hours with your team

A two hour session with you, Ava and your new digital marketing intern. We build and run your first real LinkedIn campaign together, live, so that by the end messages are going out and your team knows how to do it again on their own. In person if it suits, since Celbridge is not far.

After
Your team runs it

From then on your team runs it without me. I am on the end of an email if anyone gets stuck. When Christmas is behind you and there is room to build the rest, we pick it up from there.

What it costs you

Nothing for my time. I am doing the setup and the training at no charge. I would rather help you get this working now, and if it does, build the fuller system with you when the budget allows.

The tools you would need

Four subscriptions, in your name. Claude Code, about €100 a month, is what runs the agent, but it is also the tool you and your team will use for a lot more than this, from writing and quotes to the other agents when you come to build them, so it earns its keep well beyond the outreach. The other three are specific to the LinkedIn work: HeyReach for the sending (79 dollars a month, about €75), LinkedIn Premium (about €70 a month), and Apify for finding the right people (about €40 a month). In the region of €285 a month all in, and set against hiring someone to do this work it is small.
