LinkedIn Outbound Agent

Outbound is a job a person does. Someone has to work out which companies are worth approaching, find the right person inside each one, learn enough about that company to have something worth saying, and then write to that person properly. Done across hundreds of companies, that has traditionally meant a full-time hire, an agency, or a freelancer, and the work usually collapses into a template with a few details swapped in. The LinkedIn Outbound Agent does that whole job, and it writes every message on its own.

Your buyers are the people who look after staff and offices: heads of HR and people, office and facilities managers, and the marketing and procurement people who order branded gifts. They sit in companies across Ireland and the UK. The agent finds those people, works out which companies look like buyers right now (a company growing its headcount needs more service awards, a newly appointed head of people is reviewing how staff are recognised, a company approaching an anniversary or the Christmas season is about to order gifts), and researches each company and person before writing. Every message is written from that research, so it could only have been sent to them.

The agent runs on LinkedIn. It stops for your approval at three points: when the companies and the people are chosen, when the messages are written, and before anything is sent. You read every message first. Nothing is sent until you approve it. This is the agent you and your team would learn to run in our session. After it, it runs in your own account, on your own tools, and you run it without us.

You give the agent a single instruction, for example "find the heads of people and office managers at Irish and UK companies that look like buyers right now, and write to the right person at each". It finds that person, researches their company, and writes a message from what it found. The example below is the same business pitched to three different people.
