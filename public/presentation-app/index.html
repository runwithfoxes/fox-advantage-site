Team productivity

A coaching session, in full. A marketer brings a credible-looking brief, and the coach pressure-tests it: where the objective and KPIs sit on the ladder of marketing metrics, which of them is a commercial outcome and which isn't, and what's missing. Press the button to take each turn, and watch which rung the coaching is on. (Illustrative, not a real client.)
The ladder of marketing metrics
Every goal sits on one of these. Only the top is a commercial outcome; only the bottom is an output, not an outcome. The coach lights the rung it's working on.
Commercial
Profit · Revenue
Customer behaviour
Penetration · Frequency · Share of wallet
Memory
Salience · Consideration · Coming to mind
Communication
Reach · Engagement · Registrations
Activity
Budget spent · Impressions bought · Assets shipped
The brief measured only the bottom two rungs. The coaching walks it up.
