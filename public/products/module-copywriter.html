Copywriter

Prompting an AI for copy is fast. The copy comes back fast too, and it shows. Flat, generic, written for a brand that does not exist. Copywriter is trained on yours.

It is built on your voice guide, your messaging framework, and your past published work. When you give it a job, it writes in that voice. Web pages, emails, product copy, social captions, ad lines. And it is trained on each as its own craft, because a web headline is a different job to an email subject or a blog intro. All at volume, all sounding like you.

This is a different product from Ghostwriter, which gets your experts' thinking out of their heads and published. The Copywriter is the brand-voice workhorse for everyday marketing copy. We train it on your materials, test it until it is right, and hand it over to run in your own Claude. You approve every line before it goes anywhere.
