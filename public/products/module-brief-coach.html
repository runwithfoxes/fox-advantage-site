Brief Coach

A brief is the plan behind a piece of marketing. When the brief is weak, the work that comes out is weak too.

Brief Coach reads your brief and does what a good strategist would: it asks the hard questions until the plan is clear and worth doing. Why are we really doing this, and what would success actually look like? What do we want to be known for? What can only we say? And can the team actually make it? It uses your own goals, numbers and brand, so the questions fit your business, not generic advice. Twenty years of marketing experience, built into the questions it asks.

It pushes, but it never writes the brief for you and never just hands you a yes or no. The thinking stays yours. We build it around your business and hand it over.

A coaching session, in full. A marketer brings a credible-looking brief, and the coach pressure-tests it: where the objective and KPIs sit on the ladder of marketing metrics, which of them is a commercial outcome and which isn't, and what's missing. Press the button to take each turn, and watch which rung the coaching is on. (Illustrative, not a real client.)
The ladder of marketing metrics
Every goal sits on one of these. Only the top is a commercial outcome; only the bottom is an output, not an outcome. The coach lights the rung it's working on.

Customer behaviour

Activity

The brief measured only the bottom two rungs. The coaching walks it up.
