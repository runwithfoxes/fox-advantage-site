◆ How this works as a shared artefact

One page the whole marketing team opens inside Sabre's own Claude - always current, edited in the page itself, never emailed around as another spreadsheet version.

◆ The numbers keep themselves current

Event data, RSVP funnels and spend are pulled from your source sheet on a refresh. Nobody re-keys them, so there is one version of the truth - not a calendar that is three weeks out of date the moment it is sent.

◆ The human bits are edited in the page

Move an event, change a status, add a note, update a budget line - you do it on the page itself, with a click or a drag. It saves for everyone on the team instantly. (Try the note and the status pills above.)

◆ It lives inside Sabre, seen by Sabre only

Shared inside Sabre's own Claude organisation - authenticated to your team, not a public link. The data sits behind your login, the way the email writer and presentation tools already do.

◆ You use the page, not the chat

Day to day the team just uses the calendar - no one needs to talk to Claude or hold a licence to read it. Chat is only for when you want to redesign the board or add a new view.

What this is / isn't: a live operational view of where the marketing calendar stands - not a replacement for your planning system. Sample numbers shown here are illustrative; the real version is populated from Sabre's own event data.
