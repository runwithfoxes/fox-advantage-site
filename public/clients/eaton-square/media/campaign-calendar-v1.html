In the grid: click an event to pick it up, then click a day to move it. In the list: click a status to change it. Everything here saves for the whole team.
How it works. The calendar and statuses are kept up to date by the team here - and the live campaigns (HeyReach, Smartlead) will feed their own dates in once connected. Team notes capture what the tools can't see: a call, a reply, a heads-up.
