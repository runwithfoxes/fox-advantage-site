How to run your campaign

Start here
1 Before you message anyone
Get your list ready
You need a list of target companies, each with its website address, for example acme.com.
I already have them
Your list must be a CSV file with two columns: company name and website. If it is, go to Step 2.
I need companies
Go to clay.com and sign in. Make a new table, add the Find Companies source, set the filters (UK and Ireland, 200 to 1000 staff, your sectors), then export it as a CSV file. That CSV is your Step 2 list.
2 Once you have your raw list
Clean your list · eaton-clean
Removes any company you should not contact, and any duplicates.
Type thisIn Claude, open the shared Lists project and start a new chat. Upload your new list, then say: “Clean this list.” This runs eaton-clean.
What it does: checks your list against the team's client and do-not-contact lists, and against the lists everyone else has added, then removes any company that is already there. It matches on the website address, not the name, so “Tesco” and “Tesco Ireland” don't trip it up, and it explains every removal. This is what stops two of you messaging the same company.
You get back: the cleaned list, a “removed, and why” list, and an “unsure, your call” list. The three counts add up to what you started with.
Check: scan the “unsure” list, decide each one, then move on.
3 When your list is clean
Enrich the list · eaton-enrich
Finds the right person to contact and the signal that gives you a reason to reach out. Details only, no messages yet.
Type thisIn Claude, in the shared Lists project, stay in the same chat where you cleaned your list and say: “Now enrich the cleaned list.” (If you opened a new chat, upload the cleaned CSV first.) This runs eaton-enrich.
What it does: asks Clay for the HR leaders at each company, picks the one decision-maker by seniority, and pulls a recent HR hire as your signal. It fills in the details, name, title, LinkedIn, company and location. The messages come later, in Step 5.
You get back: your list with the contact details and the signal filled in, plus a “review, don't send” list for any company with no clear HR decision-maker.
Check: trust the review list. Anything it could not stand up, it holds back.
4 Same chat, right after Step 3
Get email addresses · eaton-email
Adds verified work emails to the list you just built.
Type thisIn Claude, in the same chat where you enriched the list, say: “Now get the emails.” This runs eaton-email.
What it does: reuses the same Clay search and fills the blank email column, verified addresses only, never a guess.
Email found
Keeps the full sequence, LinkedIn and email.
No verified email
That person runs LinkedIn only. Don't drop them, the email steps just skip.
5 When the list is enriched and emailed
Write your messages · eaton-message-coach
Coaches you through the whole sequence of messages and emails, one at a time.
Type thisIn Claude, in the shared Lists project, say: “Coach me through my message sequence.” This runs eaton-message-coach.
What it does: shows you the full journey (Message 1, Email 1, Message 2, and so on), then takes each one in turn. It shows a real company's signal, asks what you want to say, drafts it in your voice inside the limit (the LinkedIn note is 300 characters), and reworks it until you sign it off.
You get back: your approved sequence, every message signed off, ready to roll across the whole list.
Check: you are the check here. Nothing is locked until you say so.
6 Once your sequence is signed off
Build the upload file · eaton-merge
Applies your approved sequence across every lead and builds the HeyReach file.
Type thisIn Claude, in the same chat, say: “Now merge this across my list.” This runs eaton-merge.
What it does: takes your signed-off sequence and personalises each message for every company from its own signal, then builds the upload CSV in the exact shape HeyReach needs, one row per person.
You get back: the finished upload file, plus a short “spot-check these” list where the personalisation was thin. Check those few, not all of them.
7 When your upload file is ready
Load it and start the sequence · heyreach-load
Uploads your finished file into the HeyReach and SmartLead campaigns, which are already set up, then starts the sequence.
→ Runs on its own
Your 18 day sequence fires
Day 1 LinkedIn connect, then 3 emails, 3 calls with voicemails, and 3 LinkedIn messages. The cadence you built in HeyReach now runs without you.
8 Any time, on demand
Keep an eye on it · eaton-campaign-update
Get an update and your next moves whenever you want.
Type thisIn Claude, say: “Update me on the campaign.” This runs eaton-campaign-update.
What it does: reads your HeyReach and SmartLead numbers, flags what needs attention, then gives you two or three next moves.
End of the run
