The ladder, rung by rung

Read it top to bottom. Every marketing goal sits on one of these rungs. The higher the rung, the closer it is to the business actually moving.

Commercial

Profit and revenue. The only true business goal, and the one thing every campaign is ultimately judged against. Most briefs never name a number here.

The only commercial outcome

Customer behaviour

More buyers, buying more often, spending more each time. Penetration, frequency, share of wallet. This is the rung a launch campaign can genuinely own, with a figure it can be judged on.

Memory

Whether you come to mind when someone is in the market. Salience and consideration. It's the rung most launch briefs forget, and the one that tells you whether the work built anything lasting.

Communication

Reach, engagement, registrations. Real and trackable, but they only tell you people saw the work and reacted to it. They watch the campaign on the way, they aren't the goal.

Activity

Budget spent, impressions bought, assets shipped. The spend and effort going in, not an outcome coming out. Useful to track, never the measure of success.

An output, not an outcome
